# Roadmap

We are currently aligning the roadmap. Below you can find the known open feature requests, next to general stabilization and speed-up improvements. If you are interested in a specific feature, please [reach out to us](index.md#working-together).

## Open feature requests

### Reconnections

Add reconnection of disconnected lines as an additional optimization action. This extends ToOp's current action space of reassignments, station splits, disconnections and PST taps.

### Curative actions

Enable curative topology measures as an additional degree of freedom.

### Time-series optimization

- **V1:** Evaluate an optimized topology over multiple consecutive hours and determine for how long it remains beneficial.
- **V2:** Introduce true time-coupled optimization, considering dependencies between consecutive time steps.

### Visualization and frontend

- **Open-source frontend:** Open-source the frontend of ToOp. Its structure does not have to adhere to the given bulletin.
